My story

Learning today.
Building for tomorrow.

I'm Kevin Kipkemoi Ruto, a Kenyan student studying Civil Engineering at Kirinyaga University. My studies are helping me build a foundation in technical thinking, mathematics, materials, and the systems that shape the built environment.

Beyond my coursework, I'm interested in digital technology and artificial intelligence, especially how these tools can support learning, communication, and practical problem-solving. I enjoy exploring new ideas and learning by trying things, reviewing what works, and improving from feedback.

This website is a living portfolio. It will document my projects, the skills I am developing, and lessons from the process. I aim to represent my experience honestly and let the work speak for itself.
